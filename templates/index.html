The Problem

Spam is more than an annoyance

Security Risk

Phishing and scam messages disguised as legitimate texts put users at risk of fraud and data theft.

Not Scalable Manually

Filtering thousands of messages by hand is impossible — platforms need automated, real-time classification.

Poor UX Without Filtering

Unfiltered spam clutters inboxes and erodes trust in messaging platforms.

The Solution

Machine learning that classifies in milliseconds

This system uses TF-IDF text vectorization combined with a Multinomial Naive Bayes classifier to analyze message content and predict whether it's spam or legitimate — instantly, with a confidence score attached to every prediction.

How It Works

Under the hood

01

Text Vectorization (TF-IDF)

Each message is converted into a numerical representation. Words that are rare but distinctive — like "prize" or "winner" — are weighted higher than common filler words like "the" or "is."

02

Classification (Naive Bayes)

A probabilistic model trained on thousands of labeled messages calculates the likelihood a message is spam versus legitimate, based on the patterns it learned during training.

03

Prediction + Confidence Score

The system returns not just a label, but a confidence percentage — giving a clearer picture of how certain the model is, not just a binary yes/no.
